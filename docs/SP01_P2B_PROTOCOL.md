# EchoLens SP01-P2B — prospective source-level null pilot v0.2

**Prepared 2026-10-12.** Status: **protocol and source-selection software only**; no 50-star cohort has yet been acquired, no spectra newly downloaded, and no discoveries or false-alarm probabilities established. Separate from unmerged P2A PR #28. Do not merge or deploy without review.

## Scientific question
Can an uncalibrated, whole-HERMES-red-window spectral residual search be converted into a **source-level**, prospective outlier test whose false alarms and sensitivity are measured on independently selected GALAH DR4 stars? Null H0: detected residual features can result from ordinary stars, astrophysical lines, imperfect fitted stellar models, masked-pixel selection, instrument/reduction features, correlated sampling, or source matching. H1: a reproducible excess remains after registered checks; even that does **not** establish extraterrestrial technology.

## Upstream evidence, constraints and exclusions
- P2A used **3 visits of only 2 distinct stars**, Gaia DR3 3064554891927687296 (ordinary dwarf) and 6053735173729807872 (known Ba giant), whose residuals cannot establish an independent false-positive distribution. The earlier prequalified candidate Gaia 6130627011126533760 is also development-exposed. All three are prohibited from P2B confirmation cohorts. Record and quarantine all other data-exposed IDs when discovered, rather than declaring them independent.
- Data: GALAH DR4 recommended one-star-per-row allstar catalogue (`galah_dr4_allstar_240705`); allspec is per observation. IDs MUST be preserved as integer-accurate decimal strings; `float`/JavaScript rounded 64-bit Gaia IDs are forbidden. Full raw table, versions, exact catalogue query, retrieval UTC, FITS/CSV byte SHA-256 and schema metadata remain separate from the derived manifest.
- GALAH recommends `flag_sp == 0` for **trustworthy stellar-parameter reference selection** and `snr_px_ccd3 > 30`. P2B's ordinary-null eligibility additionally requires `flag_red == 0`, documented stellar-parameter/magnitude data; this conditions the null on clean processed stars and cannot describe detection completeness on flagged stars. Do NOT apply this same flag screen to the general future anomaly-search population. Do not trust `flag_fe_h` as a reliability veto due to a documented DR4 issue. Positive natural controls are reported **separately**, not added to false-positive null denominator.
- The GALAH analysis product has `wave`, `sob`, `uob`, `smod`, `mob`. `mob=True` means selected for the same-data model fit; it is NOT a native CCD cosmic-ray/bad-pixel quality bit. P2A saw substantially higher |z|>5 rates in fit-excluded pixels and strong lag-1 residual correlation. The detector-native multi-HDU reductions previously returned HTTP 403. A Data Central SSA FITS labelled G contained IR, so all camera files must pass wavelength/payload integrity checks before use.
- Wavelength calibration for DR4 reduction is in **air**; inspect the effective velocity/air frame of each derived allspec product before making physical line identifications. Use measured LSF where accessible; otherwise mark approximate R~28000 separately and perform sensitivity analysis. Never label model residual z as Gaussian detection significance.

## Locked pilot sampling plan, before any new spectra
- Target **50 different Gaia DR3 source IDs**: 40 initially ordinary FGK-dwarf reference stars and 10 independently catalogued chemically peculiar natural positives. This is a feasibility pilot, NOT an adequately powered rare-FAP claim. Previously examined stars excluded.
- Candidate-source physical screen for ordinary stars: 4800 <= Teff < 6400 K, 4.0 <= logg <= 4.65 (cgs), -0.8 <= [Fe/H] <= 0.5 dex, finite `G`, `bp_rp`, `ruwe`, `snr_px_ccd2` and `snr_px_ccd3`, `flag_sp=0`, `flag_red=0`, CCD2 and CCD3 signal-to-noise >30, `ruwe<1.4`. No missing-valued stellar-parameter substitutions. RUWE is a screening proxy and does not prove stellar singleness. For positive examples, require a separately supplied independently published classification and actual source citation; do not require clean ordinary-star flags, but record flags, photometry and SNR where available.
- Four *prospective* ordinary strata: Teff [4800,5600) vs [5600,6400) K crossed with CCD3 SNR (30,60) vs [60,+inf). Select **10 ordinary sources in each stratum** (40 total) using ascending SHA-256 of `echolens-sp01-p2b-v1:<Gaia source_id>`, not anomalies, filenames, or sky position. Within each stratum, the first five selected by hash are CALIBRATION; last five are **HOLDOUT**. For the 10 known positive examples, select by the same frozen hash from an independently referenced positive-label file; they are never used in null threshold fitting. These quotas and boundaries are operational; the selected pool may fail to provide enough stars. If any stratum or positive group is short, **stop without emitting a completed manifest** and report the shortage; don't relax rules after examining spectra.
- Source-level grouping: Gaia DR3 source ID is the immutable unit. If multiple GALAH visits exist, deterministic representative is the lowest numeric `sobject_id` for source selection; record other visits separately but they MUST share the source's role. Verify no Gaia ID is present in more than one cohort or split, and no positives entered the ordinary null. All joins of allstar to allspec must preserve IDs, visit IDs, version and provenance.
- Available Galactic latitude/sky density, parallax, extinction, photometric quality and instrument epoch distribution are *audited* after manifest freeze; their absence must be reported, not invented. The four-stratum selection is not a fully matched population. If large skews appear, publish an amendment and **new version** prospectively rather than silently reselecting this cohort. Dwarf/giant comparisons do not constitute matched controls.

## Frozen analysis before interpreting held-out stars
1. In calibration controls only, define the whole-red-window detection feature and source-level statistic: **maximum** registered robust contrast across unmasked wavelength positions (with explicit limited mask fraction). All tested positions and star-level maxima, including zero-feature sources, are recorded. Standardized spectral pixels are correlated, so empirical controls must calibrate **star-level extrema**, not per-pixel Gaussian tail areas.
2. Declare P2B engineering alert threshold from the 20 calibration maxima using a predeclared quantile/ordering rule (e.g. strict maximum exceedance) with no claim of 1% FAP. With 20 calibration stars, even a max-based nonparametric threshold is coarsely resolved; do NOT report `5-sigma` or `q<0.01` significance. A threshold must be fixed and hashed BEFORE scanning HOLDOUT spectra. Source-level and reduction-batch grouping checked; avoid duplicated star leakage.
3. Apply the frozen threshold exactly once to the 20 held-out ordinary stars; report flagged-star fraction, exact binomial uncertainty (descriptive; independence caveats), source counts, non-detections, missing spectra, failures, per-star maxima and mask fraction. Zero false alerts in 20 stars does **not** demonstrate rare-event safety (one-sided 95% upper risk is ~13.9% under independent Bernoulli assumptions).
4. Only afterwards inspect 10 known natural positives for expected chemical/model phenomena and conditional recovery. No positive is called technological. For any excursion, veto known stellar and interstellar lines, sky/telluric and instrumental artifacts; check independent epochs/instruments, wavelength/RV convention, native detector masks and realistic LSF. A star marked `mob=False` at a line is missing test coverage, not a non-detection.
5. Model fitted to the very same evaluated data is a leakage risk; P2B is **algorithm feasibility**. A future confirmation design requires independent spectral predictions or genuinely separated fit/evaluation data and an out-of-survey source-level calibration. A 50-star study is inadequate to claim astronomically small false-alarm rates.
