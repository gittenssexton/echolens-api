The original DR4 `allspec` subset independently validated two calibration source/visit IDs only, **not the entire 50-source sample**. Keep the rest provisional until checked against original release measurements and flags. Original GALAH release `allstar` is the star-level summary; original `allspec` is per-observation, and camera FITS are separate data products. No multi-GB release download has been performed.
