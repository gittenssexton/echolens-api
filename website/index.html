03 / Scientific method

Unusual is a question, not a conclusion.

What we are testing

Can uncertainty-aware comparison identify stellar deviations that survive known astrophysical and instrumental explanations?

Null hypothesis: differences arise from stellar populations, dust, extinction, binaries, variability, blending, selection effects, noise or calibration.

Alternative: a deviation replicates and remains unexplained by registered models. Even then, technological origin is not established.

How evidence stays traceable

Measurements: catalog observations, errors, correlations and flags, preserved with source identifiers.

Catalog inferences: pipeline classifications and estimated parameters, labeled as estimates.

Derived products: reproducible calculations linked to inputs and algorithm versions.

AI interpretations: proposed explanations, separate from measurements and never used to overwrite them.

How were the 10,000 sources selected?

The archived pilot uses Gaia DR3 random_index < 1,000,000 and stellar classification probability ≥ 0.9, taking the first 10,000 in random-index order through IRSA. Low signal-to-noise, missing or negative parallaxes and poor astrometric solutions remain in the registry. This is a classification-selected pilot, not a volume-complete census.

What counts as a control?

Reference candidates undergo provisional quality screening: parallax signal-to-noise ≥ 10, G < 18, astrometric solutions 31/95, RUWE < 1.4, at least nine visibility periods, no duplicate or non-single-star flag, no published VARIABLE flag, three-band flux signal-to-noise ≥ 50 and available uncertainties. Independent checks and matched stellar properties are still required. Known natural anomalies test false positives; labeled synthetic injections test sensitivity. No star is a verified lifeless control.

What must happen before an anomaly claim?

Freeze features, matching tolerances and model versions; prevent leakage across training, calibration and test groups; propagate uncertainties; calibrate empirical p-values and control multiple testing (draft FDR q ≤ 0.01). Report effect sizes and sensitivity. Require independent epochs or instruments and blinded review of natural explanations.

Why add WISE and spectroscopic surveys?

WISE will support infrared comparisons after careful crossmatching and contamination checks. Spectroscopy can test spectral residuals at the available resolution. Neither survey has yet been integrated into this pilot. Gaia broad-band observations cannot substitute for those tests.

Side project / Adversarial spectral review

Independent review, with its limits visible.

Claude’s contributed review raises sampling and spectral-model issues. Its computations and proposed explanations are attributed contributions, not independently verified stellar discoveries.

Sampling concerns

A stellar-classification probability threshold may underrepresent unusual spectral energy distributions. A future low-probability stratum should be specified before retrieval.

The pilot is predominantly faint. Before designing spectral tests, measure actual XP/RVS availability and create a suitable bright cohort. The review’s rough estimate should not substitute for a coverage query.

Proposed natural controls include chemically peculiar stars and technetium-bearing evolved stars. These are suggestions awaiting catalog selection and characterization.

Li III model decomposition · A01

Reported mean signed Ritz residual: simple H/9 scaling +0.05624%; reduced mass +0.00960%; Dirac plus reduced mass −0.00169%.

The reported maximum absolute final residual is about 0.001815%. The model explains much of the systematic offset through established atomic physics.

Retrospective: reference values were already seen before the protocol was written. It uses CODATA 2018; Atomic Spectra v4 uses CODATA 2022. These models must not be silently treated as identical.

The contribution attributes the remaining offset to a QED/Lamb-shift scale. Treat this as a proposed explanation: the listed models do not isolate or calculate every omitted contribution. A small residual against theory is not a detection.

Issues the full review identified

  • Old status text: the original handoff and reconciliation draft predate the merge. Current GitHub merge/closure records and the dated merge report take precedence.
